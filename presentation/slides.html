Engineering Risk Management

Problems Encountered & Empirical Resolutions

Risk (from Proposal) What Actually Happened Resolution / Mitigation Executed
Data Integration Complexity Disparate timestamp formats and granularity across staging sources. Standardized integer date keys (YYYYMMDD) in conformed dim_date (2020–2030).
Performance Bottlenecks High buffer cache reads (>600 shared hits) on unindexed raw staging queries. Implemented Kimball star schema with surrogate keys and materialized views (mat_monthly_sales), achieving sub-2ms response.
Scope Creep Managing complex multi-departmental business logic across 4 SME domains. Strictly bounded project scope to core decision-support KPIs for Sales, Inventory, Finance, and HR.
Data Quality Issues Synthetic HR date boundary edge cases and potential null FK risks. Engineered automated PL/pgSQL assertion suite (fn_audit_data_quality) verifying 5/5 constraints before fact loading.
Security / GDPR Breach Risk of exposing direct customer PII and sensitive employee compensation. Implemented salted SHA-256 pseudonymisation for clients and departmental aggregate rollups for HR metrics (GDPR Art. 25/32).
